## Early review of code arriving at 14:19-14:20 (in progress)

- **BLOCKER - Random-paper marks and denominators disagree.** `backend/crates/exam_grading/src/lib.rs:845`, `backend/crates/exam_grading/src/lib.rs:564`, and `backend/crates/exam_disputes/src/lib.rs:288` sum pool item marks instead of the drawn attempt's `item_marks`; `backend/crates/exam_grading/src/lib.rs:244` even sums the entire pool. For a 10-mark paper drawn from a 100-mark pool, results/certificates use different and incorrect denominators, and autograding can cap marks incorrectly. Fix all result, cap, pass and certificate calculations to use each attempt's drawn effective marks; define and test the void/pass rule, including an all-voided paper.
- **BLOCKER - Multi-select alternatives penalize original correct answers.** `backend/crates/exam_grading/src/lib.rs:808` appends alternatives to the key, while `backend/crates/examcore/src/lib.rs:271` treats every key as simultaneously required or divides marks by key size. After accepting B as an alternative to A, a previously correct A-only multi-select answer becomes partial or wrong. Represent alternatives as valid answer sets, then recompute all affected scripts with regression tests for every multi-select rule.
- **MAJOR - Blueprint tolerance is not enforced.** `backend/crates/exam_attempts/src/lib.rs:310` accepts a paper up to tolerance away from a target, so two accepted papers can differ by twice the permitted pairwise spread; it also uses the best paper after 200 tries even when none meets that test. Enforce the contract's pairwise spread under a transaction/lock against existing draws, and reject infeasible draws rather than silently exceeding tolerance; test opposite-side and infeasible cases.
- **MAJOR - AI prompt may contain candidate PII.** `backend/crates/exam_assist/src/lib.rs:75` forwards answer text and stem verbatim (apart from truncation and invisible watermark stripping). A candidate can type their name, email or phone into an answer, and an author can include personal data in a stem or rubric; the current synthetic test does not exercise this. Remove or robustly redact personal identifiers before external transmission, and test identifying strings in each prompt field; do not claim no-PII from omitting database identity columns alone.

Real browsers repeatedly disconnect instead of receiving the intended long-lived stream. Add the monitor GET to `isStream`, retain disconnect cancellation, and exercise a stream beyond 15 seconds.
