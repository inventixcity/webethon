# H11 log: inner app redesign (shell, PageHeader, examiner and candidate dashboards)

## Done
- **App shell** (`web/src/components/app-shell/app-shell.tsx`, `nav.ts`, `magnetic-cta.tsx`). Replaces `app-header.tsx`, which is deleted.
  - lg and up: fixed 248px sidebar with the mark, role badge, grouped nav with lucide icons, and a footer with the user's name and email, Settings, the theme toggle, Sign out, and a hold-to-confirm "sign out of every device" (`HoldButton`, calls `/auth/signout-all`).
  - Below lg: the sidebar becomes a focus-trapped drawer behind a menu button (Escape closes, focus returns to the button).
  - Slim top bar with a breadcrumb (`Examiner / Exams / <live exam title> / Monitor`). The exam title comes from `useExam`.
  - Content capped at 1280px with a 32px gutter on md and up (20px on phones). Single `<main id="main-content">` with a skip link.
  - Sub-nav removed from the examiner layout. Role guards unchanged.
  - Admin layout uses the same shell with admin groups plus the examiner group. `components/admin/shell.tsx` is now unused.
- **PageHeader** (`web/src/components/ui/page-header.tsx`). Compact: mono eyebrow, a clamp(32px to 48px) headline, a one-line description, actions on the right, and a thin vermilion wave hairline (WAAPI, transform only, paused off-screen and when hidden, still under reduced motion). The WaveText band is no longer used. The h1 stays accessible. The `banner` prop is now a halftone figure on the right (HalftoneField over the public/images file, with a `FIG. 01 /` caption), never behind text. `breadcrumb` is accepted but no longer rendered, since the top bar shows the trail.
- **Examiner overview** (`web/src/components/exams/examiner-home.tsx`).
  - A VelocityMarquee of live facts.
  - A SplitFlap "Live board" cycling through running exams (real title and attempt count).
  - A five-tile KPI strip with large numerals: exams, ongoing (cobalt live pulse), enrolled, attempts, and awaiting results (amber).
  - A 12-column layout: "Ongoing now" and "Upcoming" cards on the left 8 columns, with a status stripe, window, live countdown, counts, and Monitor, Edit and Preview. Preview calls `createSandboxAttempt`, as before. Drafts list.
  - Right rail: a guided flow checklist (six steps, ticked from live data, including the question bank from `/questions?limit=1`), and "Results to publish".
  - Refetches on focus and on `invigil:exam-changed` without a loading flash.
- **Candidate dashboard** (`web/src/components/candidate/candidate-dashboard.tsx`, `candidate/page.tsx`).
  - Marquee of live facts, plus a three-tile strip (open now, scheduled, completed) below the hero grid.
  - "Next up" hero, chosen from in-progress, then open, then next scheduled. Server-clock countdown in large numerals, a SplitFlap board of real facts (status, start time, duration, marks), and a magnetic Start or Resume CTA. Window, duration and marks are shown in a ruled block.
  - Upcoming cards and a Completed column. Completed cards fetch `/me/results/{attemptId}` once results are published, and show the pass or score badge, score, and a verify-code link.
  - `candidate/page.tsx` no longer wraps content in its own `<main>`.
- Effects used: velocity-marquee, split-flap (examiner live board and candidate facts board), halftone-field (header figure), magnetic-button (via `MagneticCta`), hold-button (sign out everywhere). Ink-tide not used.

## Known gaps
- "Awaiting results" uses closed or completed exams with `resultsPublishedAt` null. The list API has no grading counts, so this is not a true awaiting-grading count.
- Dashboards read only the first page of `/exams`. The KPI tile says "First page only" when there is a next cursor.
- The CTA text in vermilion (`text-paper` on `vermilion-deep`) is dark in dark mode and about 4.3:1 on the fill. Under the token rules it is not 4.5:1. Consider a dedicated on-accent token.
- The candidate and admin banner images are still being generated and may look dark at small sizes.
