SHARED STYLE: paste this at the end of EVERY prompt
---------------------------------------------------
Editorial print aesthetic, two-to-three-colour risograph / halftone grain, matte uncoated paper texture, crisp
hairline rules, generous negative space, square corners, Swiss-poster composition, high contrast, calm and premium.
Palette strictly: warm ivory #F3EEE3 (background), deep ink black #12110E, vermilion red #D23A1C, cobalt blue
#2B4FD8, amber orange #E0862C. Use at most two accent colours per image, in small amounts. No gradients, no glow,
no glassmorphism, no purple, no neon, no 3D render look. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks.
No close-up faces.
