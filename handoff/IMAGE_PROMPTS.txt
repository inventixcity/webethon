INVENTIX INVIGIL: IMAGE PROMPTS
================================
Generate each image, save it with the EXACT file name shown, and drop all of them into:
    web/public/images/
Then tell the lead "images added". The lead converts them to .webp, sizes them and wires them in.

Shared style (already included in every prompt, so don't change it):
- The palette is strict: warm ivory paper (#F3EEE3), deep ink black (#12110E), and ONE small vermilion red accent (#D23A1C).
  No other colours, no purple, no blue, no gradients, no glow, no glassmorphism.
- Editorial print look: a fashion-magazine / Swiss-poster feel, risograph or halftone grain, crisp hairline rules,
  generous negative space, square corners, high contrast, matte paper texture.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks in the image (the UI adds its own type).
- No people's faces in close-up (privacy theme). Hands, silhouettes, objects and spaces are fine.
