## 1. Contact page: engaging, interactive, animated, still accessible and fast
- **Split layout on desktop:**
  - **Left:** a bold headline ("Bring Invigil to your institute."), 3 animated proof points (e.g. "Offline-proof exams", "Signed, verifiable results", "Live control room"), the response-time promise, and the direct contact details.
  - **Right:** the form.
- **Form as a short stepped flow (3 steps) with a progress rail:**
  1. Institute and size: institute name, and expected candidates as a slider or segmented chips (e.g. <100 / 100–500 / 500–2k / 2k+), mapped to a number.
  2. Who you are: name, role chips (Exam controller / Faculty / IT / Admin / Other), email, and optional phone.
  3. What you need: message, with helpful prompt chips that insert starter text.
  - Next and Back buttons, Enter to advance, validation per step with inline errors, and focus moved to the step heading.
  - A live preview card ("Your request") that fills as they type.
- **Micro-interactions:**
  - floating or animated labels
  - fields get a cobalt underline sweep on focus
  - chips have the fill-wipe on select
  - the progress rail fills smoothly
  - the submit button shows a loading state
- **Success state:** an animated confirmation (an ASCII or line-drawn envelope animation, or a stamp landing), the request summary, and "We'll reply to <email> within one working day". Errors go through the toast stack.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Keep the honeypot field hidden. It must fit one laptop screen (1366x768) and look great at 390px.

## 2. Footer
Today, in dark mode the dark footer blends into the dark page and looks flat. Make it distinct and polished in BOTH themes:
- In dark mode, use a slightly raised black (e.g. #161512) with a top hairline in cobalt or ivory at low opacity, a subtle grid or halftone texture, and clear paper-coloured text with muted secondary text at ≥ 4.5:1.
- In light mode, an ink band.
- A big CTA strip at the top of the footer: "Run your next exam on Invigil" with Contact us (primary) and Verify a result (secondary).
- Columns, a contact block, and the bottom bar with ©.
- Square corners and hairline rules, with no clutter. It stacks nicely on mobile.
